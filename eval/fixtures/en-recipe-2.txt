Skillet cornbread

Cornbread baked in a hot cast-iron skillet gets a crisp, golden edge that you just can't get from a regular baking tin. This version is only lightly sweet, so it works just as well next to a bowl of chili as it does with butter and honey for breakfast.

Makes ⟦8⟧ wedges

Ingredients
- ⟦180⟧ g cornmeal
- ⟦125⟧ g all-purpose flour
- ⟦2⟧ tablespoons sugar
- ⟦2⟧ teaspoons baking powder
- ⟦1⟧ teaspoon salt
- ⟦2⟧ eggs
- ⟦350⟧ ml buttermilk
- ⟦60⟧ g butter, plus a little extra for the pan

Put the skillet in the oven and heat it to ⟦220⟧ °C. Letting the pan heat up with the oven is the secret to a good crust.

In a large bowl, mix the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. In another bowl, whisk the eggs with the buttermilk. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly.

Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones, add the melted butter and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine; if you overmix, the cornbread will turn out tough.

Carefully take the hot skillet out of the oven, add a small knob of butter and swirl it around. It should sizzle straight away. Pour in the batter and bake for ⟦20⟧ to ⟦25⟧ minutes, until the top is golden and a skewer comes out clean.

Let it cool in the pan for five minutes before cutting it into wedges.
